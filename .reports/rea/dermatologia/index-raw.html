Popular Questions

How does Lipedema Guru help me understand lipedema?

We offer research-based insights to clarify your body’s signals, like hormonal triggers, helping you navigate lipedema symptoms with knowledge and resilience.

Are consultations in-person or virtual?

All consultations are virtual via Zoom or Google Meets, ensuring accessibility and convenience with emailed details.

What options are available for General Consultations?

Choose a 1-hour session with a personalized lipedema report or one with Bioresonance Hair Testing (IgG-based), including an additional sensitivity report and 30-minute follow-up.

How do I know if the Pre- and Post-Surgery Consultation is right for me?

If you’re preparing for or recovering from lipedema surgery, this consultation provides education to manage symptoms, tailored to your surgical phase.

What is the Lipedema Guru report?

A report based on the pre-consultation quiz and consultation offers tailored insights on managing lipedema symptoms, created with consented PII/PHI data.

Do I need to consent for the consultation?

Consent for PII/PHI use is required for the personalized report but optional for a basic consultation without reports.

Can General Consultations diagnose lipedema?

No, consultations and IgG Tests provide CAM-based education, not diagnoses. Consult a healthcare provider for medical diagnosis.

How does the consultation support daily life?

Education on nutrition, movement, and mindset helps you manage lipedema symptoms, fostering resilience to thrive in daily activities.
